Formal City Council actions, item-specific public comment, and recorded votes - beginning with the current council.

How this record works

Every action item is logged, including those with no public comment. Comments count toward an item only when made during that item’s official public-comment or public-hearing block. General remarks made elsewhere in the meeting are not assigned to a vote.
