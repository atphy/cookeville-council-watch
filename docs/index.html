Other means an item-specific question, procedural concern, or mixed/unclear remark—not a clear request to approve or reject. Only remarks within an item’s official public-comment or public-hearing block are counted.

Method & sources

Scope: This record covers the current Cookeville City Council, seated August 20, 2026. It does not cover earlier councils.

Every formal action is logged, including no-comment items. Source minutes, agendas, and transcripts are archived with the project. General remarks elsewhere in a meeting are never assigned to a vote.
