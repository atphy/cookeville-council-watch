Hold a public hearing and consider on first reading Ordinance #026-
09-16, rezoning a portion of property located on Dogwood Circle and
Forrest Road (Tax Map 027, portion of Parcel 127.00) from RS-15,
"Single-Family Residential" to RS-10, "Single-Family Residential".

     Additional information: Appendix 7B

     Several residents spoke in opposition to the increased density that
     Ordinance #026-09-16 would allow, raising concerns about flooding,
     sinkholes, road safety, and unwanted changes to an established
     neighborhood that increased density would bring.

     City Council discussion explored the requirements for stormwater
     mitigation that would be required, the possible number of houses that
     would be allowed under the RS-10 zoning designation, as well as the
     appropriateness of the rezoning in compliance with the 2030 Plan and
     the City Zoning Code.

     Councilman England made a motion to approve on first reading
     Ordinance #026-09-16. The motion was seconded by Vice-Mayor
     Eldridge. Upon call for a vote, the motion carried with the following
     voting:

                  Councilman Bagci:          aye
                  Councilman England:        aye
                  Mayor Gilbert:             aye
                  Vice-Mayor Eldridge:       aye
                  Councilman Parkison:       aye
